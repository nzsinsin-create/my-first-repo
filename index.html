The Story

Founded on a calling.

Giacomo Berselli grew up in the Italian countryside, spending his summers on his aunt's farm. After founding two IT companies in the 1990s, he followed what he describes as a calling toward the role of ambassador for Italian food and wine culture. In 2005, he established the Marco Polo Program to bring Italian culinary education to American institutions. In 2014, he relocated to New York to build U.S. partnerships directly. Twenty years across two continents have produced lasting relationships with Italian producers, regional institutions, and American culinary schools.

"Sharing Italian food and wine passion and Italian culture can be a pillar in students' education."
— Giacomo Berselli, Founder

    What we are

    Marco Polo is an academic partner, cultural bridge, and system-based learning provider — premium, limited-access, and institution-backed. Every program is built in direct collaboration with your faculty, around documented learning outcomes, delivered by Italian industry professionals with relationships built since 2005. This is a structured academic immersion program, not a travel tour — credit-eligible, institution-backed, and built around what your department actually needs.
