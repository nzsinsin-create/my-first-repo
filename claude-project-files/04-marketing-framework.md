# Marketing Framework (Our Anchor)

## 1. Psychology basics
- **System 1 / System 2 (Kahneman):** scrollers decide fast and emotionally first, then justify with logic.
  Hook the feeling first, then give clear facts.
- **Cialdini's levers (used ethically):**
  - Reciprocity - give real value for free (guides, checklists, explainers).
  - Authority - show the doctor's credentials and expertise.
  - Social proof - real, consented testimonials about the experience (never medical result promises).
- **Cognitive ease:** clean layouts, one idea per post, simple words.
- **Loss aversion (handled honestly):** remove uncertainty about price, process and expectations.
  Reduce fear of the unknown; never make real risks sound smaller than they are.

## 2. Funnel
| Stage | Format | Focus | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| TOFU (awareness) | Reels / TikToks | Relatable pain point | Shares, reach |
| MOFU (trust) | Carousels, guides | Useful education, doctor authority | Saves |
| BOFU (action) | Offer posts, process videos | Price and process transparency | DMs, bookings |

## 3. Awareness stages
1. **Problem-aware** - feels the symptoms -> TOFU relatable content.
2. **Solution-aware** - knows help exists -> MOFU expert education.
3. **Most aware** - knows us, hesitant -> BOFU transparency: price, process, what to expect.

## 4. Content pillars
1. **Body basics** - simple science of metabolism, hormones, skin, recovery.
2. **Evidence vs. hype** - how to tell real research from trends and anecdotes.
3. **Weight-management literacy** - assessment, realistic expectations, safety.
4. **Quality and verification** - how to check FDA registration (verification.fda.gov.ph), licenses, red flags.
5. **Affordability and access** - real costs, questions to ask a doctor.
6. **Sustainable habits** - nutrition, movement, sleep, stress, routines.

## 5. Weekly mix (starting point)
- 2 TOFU reels, 2 MOFU carousels, 1 BOFU post, plus Stories.

## 6. Growth mechanics
- **Hook in the first 1.5 seconds:** specific local detail, bold statement or relatable moment.
- **Comment-to-DM:** use neutral keywords (GUIDE, CHECKLIST, BOOK). Never ask people to share health details in comments.
- **Links:** use link in bio, Story links or DMs instead of caption links.

## 7. Example ideas
- TOFU: "POV: you sleep 8 hours and still crash at 2 PM."
- MOFU: "What a proper weight or PCOS assessment actually checks" (7-slide carousel).
- BOFU: "What happens when you book a consultation with our doctor" (walk-through video).
