# Audience Personas and Pain Points

## Pain-point hypotheses (to validate with 12-15 interviews)
These are guesses, not proven facts. Update this file as real customer answers come in.
1. Affordability and total treatment cost
2. Conflicting health information online
3. Worry about product authenticity and quality
4. Fear of side effects
5. Not knowing if they qualify or which options exist
6. Privacy and embarrassment when talking about weight or health

## Persona A - The Informed Researcher
- Follows weight-loss and wellness discussions, has read about GLP-1 medicines.
- Wants: evidence, sources, clear costs, a legitimate doctor.
- Content: evidence summaries, "how to check if a product is FDA-registered", what a proper assessment includes.

## Persona B - The Cautious Explorer
- Has heard about new weight and wellness treatments; worried about safety.
- Wants: simple explanations, reassurance that a doctor is involved, clear next steps.
- Content: beginner guides, FAQs, "what happens in a consultation", balanced safety education.

## Persona C - The Zero-Knowledge Audience
- Struggles with weight, PCOS, fatigue or skin; doesn't know what options exist.
- Wants: to feel understood, basic explanations, a first easy step.
- Content: relatable problem posts, basic health literacy, sleep/food/movement tips.

## Interview questions (for validation)
1. What's the hardest part about managing your weight / PCOS / energy right now?
2. What have you already tried? What happened?
3. Where do you look for health information? Who do you trust?
4. What worries you most about trying a new treatment?
5. What would make you comfortable booking a doctor consultation?
6. What price range feels reasonable for a consultation? For a 3-month program?
7. How private do you want this to be? How would you prefer to be contacted?

## Audience we do NOT target
- Minors (under 18)
- People with signs of an eating disorder for weight-loss offers. For them, content is supportive only and points to professional help.
