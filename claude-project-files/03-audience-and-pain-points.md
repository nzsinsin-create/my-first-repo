# Audience and Pain Points

## Core customer
Filipino women 18+, mostly 25-40, trying to lose weight, who also want clearer skin and steady energy.
Busy (work, family, studies), have tried diets before, scroll TikTok and Facebook for health tips.

## Focus areas (in order)
1. Weight
2. Energy
3. Skin

## Pain-point hypotheses (validate with interviews and DMs)
1. "I've tried everything and nothing sticks." Diet fatigue
2. "Healthy food is expensive and hassle." Cost and time
3. "I don't know who to believe online." Conflicting info
4. "I'm scared of fake or unsafe products." Trust, after all the news about illegal weight-loss drugs
5. "I'm always tired, even after sleeping." Energy
6. "My skin is dull / breaking out." Skin
7. "I'm embarrassed to talk about my weight." Privacy and shame

## Personas
### A - The Researcher ("Bea", 32, Makati office worker)
Reads about GLP-1s and wellness trends. Wants evidence, legitimacy and clear prices.
Content: evidence vs hype, how to verify FDA registration, what a proper doctor assessment includes.

### B - The Cautious Explorer ("Joy", 28, Cavite, work-from-home)
Curious but worried about safety. Wants simple explanations and a doctor in the loop.
Content: beginner guides, FAQs, "what happens when you start &well".

### C - The Busy Beginner ("Mae", 36, mom of two)
Knows little; just wants to feel and look better. Wants a simple, affordable first step.
Content: relatable POVs, easy Filipino meals, 5-minute habits, energy tips.

## Interview / DM questions
1. What's the hardest part about losing weight for you right now?
2. What have you already tried? What happened?
3. Where do you get health tips? Who do you trust?
4. What worries you most about trying something new?
5. Would ₱1,899-₱2,999 a month for a plan and coaching feel worth it? Why or why not?
6. How do you prefer to be contacted: WhatsApp or Messenger?

## Audience we do NOT target
- Minors (under 18)
- People showing signs of an eating disorder. For them, content is supportive only and points to professional help.
