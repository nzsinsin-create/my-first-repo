# Launch Plan: 7-Day Sprint + First 30 Days (organic, ₱0)

Launching in about a week, so this is a soft launch: start simple, improve as you go.

## Days 1-2: Set up
- Canva brand kit: logo, colors, fonts, 5 templates (quote post, carousel, Story, reel cover, offer post).
- Same handle on FB, IG and TikTok: @andwell[.ph] (check availability).
- Bio: "Meal plans & wellness protocols for Filipinas 🤍 Weight • Energy • Skin | Doctor-guided | Nationwide"
- WhatsApp Business (free): greeting message, away message, catalog with the 2 offers, quick replies.
- Messenger auto-replies with FAQ buttons.
- Google Form for the waitlist.

## Days 3-4: Free guide + first content
- Build the free guide in Canva (see "Lead magnet" below).
- Batch-film B-roll: meal prep, water, morning routine, skincare, walking, grocery shopping.
- Make 3 posts and 2 videos.

## Days 5-6: Pre-launch posting
- Post a "Something new is coming" teaser + founder voiceover video ("Bakit ko sinimulan ang &well").
- Post the free guide offer: "Comment GUIDE."
- DM 20-30 friends and contacts who fit the audience; ask for honest feedback and shares.

## Day 7: Soft launch
- Post the offer: what's included, prices, how it works, how to message.
- Founding-member offer: first month of &well Protocol at ₱2,499 for the first 10 sign-ups.
- Personally message everyone on the waitlist.

## Goal
10-20 waitlist sign-ups / DMs by launch day, 3-5 paying founding members in the first 30 days.

## Lead magnet (free guide)
**"The &well Weight Reset Starter Guide"** (8-10 Canva pages):
1. Why crash diets backfire (simple science)
2. The &well plate: how to build a Filipino meal for weight, skin and energy
3. 7 easy Filipino meal ideas (budget-friendly)
4. Do's and don'ts for the first 30 days
5. Energy habits: sleep, water, movement
6. Skin-friendly foods
7. Thinking about weight-loss medicines? 5 safety questions to ask a doctor, and how to check FDA
   registration at verification.fda.gov.ph
8. Next step: join &well

## Days 8-30
- Post on the solo cadence (file 05).
- Turn the "complications of unmanaged weight" infographic into a carousel (doctor-checked facts).
- Collect feedback from founding members, then make a testimonial plan (with consent).
