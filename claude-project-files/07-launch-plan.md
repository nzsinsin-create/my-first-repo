# Startup Launch Plan (organic, ₱0)

## Phase 1: Foundations (Weeks 1-2)
- Finalize the brand kit in Canva: logo, colors, fonts, 5 post templates.
- Set up FB Page, IG and TikTok with the same name, bio and profile photo.
- Sign the written partner-doctor agreement.
- Run 12-15 customer interviews (questions in file 03). Update the pain points with what you learn.

## Phase 2: Pre-launch audience building (Weeks 3-6)
- Post on the solo cadence (file 05).
- Content focus: founder story ("why I started this"), relatable pain points, free education.
- Free lead magnet, e.g. "Weight & PCOS Doctor-Visit Prep Checklist", given via comment-to-DM.
- Waitlist via Google Form. Goal: [number] sign-ups.
- Engage daily for 15 minutes: reply to comments, comment in local wellness and PCOS communities
  (helpfully, no spam).

## Phase 3: Launch (Week 7)
- Announce the launch: what we offer, how the doctor partnership works, prices, how to book.
- Message the waitlist first, with an early-bird offer on coaching (not on medicines).
- Do a TikTok/IG Live Q&A with the partner doctor if they agree.

## Phase 4: Grow (Week 8+)
- Ask happy clients for consented testimonials about their experience.
- Double down on the posts with the most saves and DMs.
- Review numbers every 2 weeks and adjust.
