# Competitor and Market Notes (researched Nov 2026; check again before relying on it)

## Market context
- Philippine authorities and doctors are actively cracking down on unregistered weight-loss drugs:
  - The Philippine College of Physicians warned the public about compounded and unregistered GLP-1 drugs,
    including from online sellers and telehealth vendors (Mar 2026).
  - Two people were arrested in Quezon City for selling unregistered tirzepatide (Mar 2026).
  - Zuellig Pharma said Mounjaro KwikPen is the only FDA-approved tirzepatide in the Philippines (Apr 2026).
  - Senators questioned the FDA over rampant online sales of unregistered health products (Jan 2026).
- Takeaway: customers are worried about fake and unsafe products. A brand that is visibly safe and legitimate stands out.

## &you (andyou), the closest competitor
- Licensed telehealth platform: weight loss, hair loss, sexual health.
- Online doctor consultation; medicines prepared by a licensed partner pharmacy, delivered discreetly nationwide.
- Trust signals: "As seen on" press, certificates and permits page, clinical-trial numbers, member stats.
- Separate landing pages for women and men.
- Lessons: show permits and legitimacy openly, make the process feel simple, use separate pages per audience.
- **Name note:** "&well" looks very similar to "&you". Check IPOPHL for trademark conflicts before investing in the brand.

## Regenerative / longevity clinics (premium, Metro Manila)
Hara Clinic, Villa Medica (BGC), Owen Regenerative & Longevity (QC), Raya Clinic (Makati), Aegle Wellness Center (Makati).
