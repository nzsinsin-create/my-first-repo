# Platform Playbook

## Facebook - community and information
- Educational posts, FAQs, moderated discussions, clinic info.
- Cadence: 3 posts per week.
- Tone: warmer, longer captions, Taglish OK.

## Instagram - brand look and recall
- Carousels, Reels, Stories (polls, Q&A, booking link).
- Cadence: 3 feed posts per week + Stories most days.
- Keep the grid visually consistent with the brand kit.

## TikTok - discovery
- Short explainers, myth-busting, day-in-the-clinic, doctor Q&A, relatable POVs.
- Cadence: 2-5 videos per week.
- Note: TikTok bans prescription-medicine ads in the Philippines. Content is education and clinic services only.

## Repurposing
One topic -> 1 reel + 1 carousel + 1 Facebook post + 2-3 Stories. Adapt the hook and length for each platform.

## What we measure
Saves, shares, video completion, comments with questions, DMs, consultations booked.
Views alone don't count as success.
