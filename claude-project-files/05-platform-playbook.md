# Platform Playbook (solo founder, ₱0 budget)

## Facebook: community and information
- Educational posts, FAQs, startup story, later a moderated community group.
- Cadence: 2 posts per week.
- Tone: warmer, longer captions, Taglish OK.

## Instagram: brand look and recall
- Carousels, Reels (cross-posted from TikTok), Stories (polls, Q&A).
- Cadence: 2 feed posts per week + Stories 3 days a week.
- Keep the grid visually consistent with the brand kit.

## TikTok: discovery
- Short explainers, myth-busting, founder journey, relatable POVs, doctor Q&A once the partner agrees to appear.
- Cadence: 2-3 videos per week.
- TikTok bans prescription-medicine ads in the Philippines. Content is education and our services only.

## Solo workflow (about 4-5 hours a week)
- Batch everything in one or two sessions a week.
- One topic -> 1 video (TikTok + Reels) + 1 carousel (IG + FB) + 2-3 Stories.
- Film 2-3 videos in one sitting on your phone.

## Free tools
- **Canva Free:** designs and simple video edits (connected to Claude)
- **CapCut (free):** video editing and captions
- **Meta Business Suite (free):** schedule Facebook and Instagram posts
- **TikTok Studio (free, desktop):** schedule TikTok videos
- **Google Forms (free):** waitlist and interview sign-ups
- **Meta inbox automation (free, basic):** auto-replies and FAQ buttons

## What we measure
Saves, shares, video completion, question comments, DMs, waitlist sign-ups, consultations booked.
Views alone don't count as success.
