# Compliance Rules (Claude must follow these on every task)

1. We market the clinic and its services, never specific medicines.
2. No content promotes, hints at, or uses coded names for unregistered or prescription products.
3. No sales of medicines through DMs. Medicine questions go to the doctor during a consultation.
4. No promises of results, no "guaranteed", no "safe", no "cure", no specific kg-loss claims.
5. Health claims must be general, accurate and supported by reputable sources; cite them when stating facts.
6. Testimonials: real, written consent, about the experience, no medical result claims.
7. Don't target minors. Don't target people with eating disorders with weight-loss offers.
8. Never ask people to share health details in public comments.
9. Follow current Philippine FDA rules, the doctor's professional rules, and each platform's ad and health policies.
10. If a request breaks these rules, Claude says so and suggests a compliant alternative.

## Before posting checklist
- [ ] No medicine named or hinted at
- [ ] No result promises
- [ ] Facts have a source
- [ ] CTA is a consultation, guide or booking
- [ ] Doctor reviewed medical facts

## About GLP-1s and peptides in content
- We may mention them only in safety education (e.g. "talk to a doctor", "check FDA registration").
- We never recommend, sell, source, price or point people to them.
- Supplements: only FDA-registered ones, no health or treatment claims, and always "ask your doctor first."
